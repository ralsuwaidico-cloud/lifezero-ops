# OWNER QUEUE — Etsy listing for the Reseller Inventory & Profit Tracker

**Status: prepared, not listed. Nothing here has been published anywhere.**

**Why this needs you:** opening an Etsy shop requires a real identity, a bank account and a card.
I cannot do any of it. What I can do is remove every other hour of work, which is what this file
is — the title, the thirteen tags, the description, the price reasoning and the file to upload are
all below, written to be pasted.

**Your time:** ~30 minutes for shop setup (identity, bank, payment), then ~20 minutes for the
listing itself. One time. The listing then sits there.

**Cost:** $0.20 per listing, renewed every four months if it has not sold. Etsy takes 6.5% of the
sale plus payment processing (about 3% + $0.25 for US sellers, varies by country). On a $19 sale
that is roughly $19.00 − $1.24 − $0.82 − $0.20 ≈ **$16.74 net**, against $16.60 on Gumroad after
its 10% + 50¢. The economics are the same. A one-time shop set-up fee may be charged in some
countries (my earlier note said $15 for the UAE — worth confirming at signup, and it does not
change the decision).

## Why this is now the highest-value thing on this list — and why I was wrong to reject it

On 2026-09-12 I put Etsy under "Rejected for now — $15 to open a shop, revisit after the first
revenue." That was the wrong call, made on cost rather than on where the buyers are, and today's
evidence reverses it.

I searched for our exact category, with the word *gumroad* in the query:
`reseller profit tracker spreadsheet gumroad ebay poshmark mercari`. Nine results came back:

- **five Etsy listings**
- one Indie Hackers post, two independent blogs, one YouTube video
- **zero gumroad.com product pages** — not ours, and not the established competitor that Gumroad's
  own Discover index says exists (*Bulk Buy Profit Calculator Spreadsheet | Reseller Profit
  Tracker*, $9.99, `resaleresources.gumroad.com`).

That last point is the one that matters. A competitor with a real, indexed, selling Gumroad product
does not appear either. So this is not about our page being new or our copy being weak — **Gumroad
product pages are not competing in this index at all, and Etsy owns the query.** Every hour spent
making our Gumroad listing more searchable is an hour spent in the wrong marketplace. Etsy is not
"another channel to try later"; on this evidence it is the channel.

Etsy also has its own internal search, which is the actual prize: buyers there are already looking
to buy a spreadsheet, rather than being shown one.

**Expected value: the first sale, and more importantly the first review.** I will not put a
revenue number on it that I cannot support — I could not read live Etsy prices (etsy.com is blocked
from my environment) and I have no impression data from anywhere yet. What I can say is that this
is the only channel identified in eight days where the buyers demonstrably are, that it costs under
a dollar a month to stay in, and that a first review there is worth more than the sale, because it
is also what unlocks Gumroad Discover for everything else on that storefront.

## The listing

**Product to list:** the $19 Reseller Inventory & Profit Tracker only. Not the UAE tracker — UAE
Corporate Tax is not a thing Etsy buyers search for, and one listing is one variable.

**File to upload:** `products/reseller-profit-tracker/Reseller_Inventory_Profit_Tracker_2026.xlsx`
in this repo. Etsy allows up to five files; upload that one. Consider also uploading
`products/reseller-free-calculator/Reseller_Profit_After_Fees_Calculator_2026.xlsx` as a bonus
file in the same listing — it costs nothing, it is already built, and "two sheets, not one" is a
real reason to pick this listing over the identical-looking ones next to it.

**Title** (Etsy allows 140 characters; the first ~40 are what show in search results):

> Reseller Inventory & Profit Tracker 2026 | eBay Poshmark Mercari Depop Whatnot Fee Calculator | Excel & Google Sheets | Schedule C Tax

**The thirteen tags** (Etsy gives you 13, max 20 characters each — all of these fit):

```
reseller spreadsheet
ebay profit tracker
poshmark tracker
mercari spreadsheet
depop seller
inventory tracker
profit calculator
resale tracker
flipping spreadsheet
schedule c template
google sheets
small business
thrift flipping
```

**Description** (paste as-is; Etsy shows the first two lines before "read more", so they carry the
whole pitch):

> See what you actually keep after eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Depop and Whatnot take their cut.
> Every fee rate in this sheet carries the date it was last checked, so you can see what has been
> verified and what has not.
>
> WHAT IT DOES
> • Inventory with auto-generated SKUs, source, brand, cost and extra costs
> • Status for every item: Sourced, Listed, Sold, Returned
> • Real profit per item after platform fees, shipping label and supplies
> • ROI and days-to-sell, so you can see which sourcing actually pays
> • Dead stock flagged by how long it has sat
> • Monthly profit summary, and a Schedule C tax summary for filing
>
> THE FEE MATHS, SPELLED OUT
> The fee table is editable and every row carries the date it was last checked. Current as of
> September 2026: eBay 13.6% + $0.40 per order ($0.30 on orders of $10 or less), Poshmark a flat
> $2.95 under $15 and 20% at or above it, Mercari 10% with no seller processing fee since January
> 2025, Depop 3.3% + $0.45 processing with no US commission, Facebook Marketplace 10% with an
> $0.80 minimum. If one of those changes, you change one cell.
>
> WHAT IT IS NOT
> It is not an app, it does not connect to your accounts, and there is no subscription. It is a
> spreadsheet that does the arithmetic correctly. If you already have a system that tells you your
> real profit per item, you do not need this.
>
> COMPATIBILITY
> Excel 2016 or later, and Google Sheets. No macros, so nothing to enable and nothing your
> workplace will block.
>
> INSTANT DOWNLOAD. Nothing ships.

**Price: list at $19.** Deliberately the same as Gumroad, so that if it sells on Etsy and not on
Gumroad, the difference is the marketplace and not the price — one variable. I could not read live
Etsy prices to build a comps table (etsy.com is blocked from my environment), so treat $19 as a
starting point rather than a researched number. **Price is the obvious second test** once there is
any traffic, and by then it can be tested against real data instead of guessed at.

**Do not link to Gumroad from the Etsy listing.** Etsy prohibits directing buyers off-site, and a
policy strike on a brand-new shop would cost more than the traffic is worth. That also means there
is no UTM link for this experiment — attribution comes from Etsy's own sales data, which is
cleaner anyway.
