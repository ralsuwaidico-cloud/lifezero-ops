## Why it is withdrawn

The ask was: add `community.n8n.io` to the egress allowlist so the Acquisition Desk could read the
Jobs category's rules and then its buyer briefs. **R&D has now obtained the rules without the egress
line, through a search engine, and they close the route on both limbs.**

Two clauses from the **n8n Community Terms of Service** (`community.n8n.io/tos`), surfaced by two
independent searches and corroborated by a forum thread in which a user quotes the same provision:

> *"You may not send advertisements, chain letters, or other solicitations through the forum, or use
> the forum to gather addresses or other personal data for commercial mailing lists or databases."*

> *"You may not automate access to the forum, or monitor the forum, such as with a web crawler,
> browser plug-in or add-on, or other computer program that is not a web browser. You may crawl the
> forum to index it for a publicly available search engine, if you run one."*

**Both limbs of R-071 are forbidden:**

| What R-071 required | ToS |
|---|---|
| Replying to buyer threads with a priced offer | **A solicitation. Forbidden by clause 1.** |
| An agent reading the buyer feed or drafting replies | **Automated access. Forbidden by clause 2.** |

The single exemption in clause 2 is crawling to index for a **publicly available search engine**,
which LIFE ZERO does not run.

**Evidence grade: REPORTED.** The clause text came from search results citing the primary ToS page.
R&D did not fetch that page — the host is egress-blocked, and **fetching it automatically would itself
