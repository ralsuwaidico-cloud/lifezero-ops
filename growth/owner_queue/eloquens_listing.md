## Why Eloquens and not Etsy for this one

Yesterday's finding was that Etsy owns the reseller-spreadsheet query and Gumroad is nowhere in it.
Today I ran the same control test on the UAE side, and **the answer is different**, which is the
whole reason it was worth running rather than assuming.

Searching `UAE corporate tax VAT return spreadsheet template excel freelancer small business
download` returns nine results: specialist UAE accounting and tax-template sites (Bespoke
Accounting, One Desk Solution, ExcelDataPro, excelaccountingtemplate.com, Daftra, smallerp.ae), a
Freelancer.co.uk project listing — and **Eloquens**, carrying a directly comparable product:

> *UAE VAT201 Return Tracker — FTA-Compliant Excel Template | 13-Box | Auto-Calculated |
> 2025–2027*, by Hoda Elmorshidy

**Zero gumroad.com results. And zero Etsy results.** So the two halves of this business need two
different marketplaces, and shipping the UAE tracker to Etsy — which was the obvious next move
after yesterday — would have been the wrong one. Etsy is a craft-and-printables marketplace; UAE
tax compliance buyers are not there. Eloquens is a finance-and-accounting template marketplace, and
it is the only general marketplace that ranks in this category at all.

**File:** `products/uae-vat-tracker/UAE_Business_Bookkeeping_VAT_CT_Tracker_2026.xlsx`.

**Title:**

> UAE VAT 201 & Corporate Tax Tracker 2026 — Excel & Google Sheets for Freelancers and Small
> Businesses | VAT Return Boxes, CT Estimate, Small Business Relief Check

**Category:** Finance → Accounting (the same category the comparable tracker sits in).

**Description** (paste as-is):

> One spreadsheet for UAE VAT and Corporate Tax. Log your invoices and expenses once, through the
> year, and your VAT 201 boxes and your Corporate Tax estimate fill themselves in — so that when
> the return is due, the numbers already exist.
>
> WHAT IT DOES
> • Income sheet — one row per invoice, VAT treatment picked from a dropdown (Standard 5% /
>   Zero-rated / Exempt / Out of scope), paid / unpaid / partially paid, days outstanding and an
>   OVERDUE flag.
> • Expenses sheet — receipts, whether input VAT is recoverable, whether the cost is deductible
>   for Corporate Tax.
> • VAT Return helper — set your FTA tax-period dates and it totals standard-rated, zero-rated and
>   exempt supplies, recoverable input VAT and net VAT payable or refundable, laid out like the
>   return boxes so you can copy them into EmaraTax.
> • VAT registration check — rolling 12-month taxable turnover against the AED 375,000 mandatory
>   and AED 187,500 voluntary thresholds.
> • Corporate Tax estimate — the 0% band at AED 375,000, the 9% rate, irrecoverable VAT, manual
>   adjustments, and a Small Business Relief eligibility check (revenue ≤ AED 3,000,000, periods
>   ending on or before 31 December 2029 — extended from 2026 by Ministerial Decision 131 of 2026).
>   Shows a suggested monthly set-aside.
> • Dashboard — revenue, expenses, profit and cash by month, receivables ageing, VAT due and CT
>   estimate tiles.
>
> EVERY RATE CARRIES THE DATE IT WAS LAST CHECKED
> The Settings sheet holds every threshold and rate in one place, each with its legal source and
> the date it was last verified, so you can see what has been checked and what has not. That is
> also how you update it yourself if the law moves.
>
> DETAILS
> 500 rows per sheet, dropdowns throughout, blue = you type / black = formula, sample rows
> included. Microsoft Excel 2016 or later and Google Sheets. No macros.
>
> NOT TAX ADVICE. This is a bookkeeping aid. Confirm your obligations with the FTA (tax.gov.ae) or
> a registered tax agent.
