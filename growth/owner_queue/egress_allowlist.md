access level, or add named hosts to the allowed domains.

## Why

capital allocation rests on it. R&D tested four independent routes on 2026-09-25 — WebFetch to job
boards, direct HTTPS to 24 hosts, the GitHub search API, repo-scoped GitHub — and all are blocked.
Full map: `org/REACHABILITY.md`.

The Acquisition Desk has spent 45 runs screening venues precisely because it cannot measure demand.

failure of the Desk; it is a network setting.

## What it does and does not buy

**Does:** restores demand measurement, so the 70/20/10 split can be set on current evidence instead
of a frozen week-old count. Directly unblocks experiment rd-2, which is otherwise permanently dead.

**Does not:** open a sales venue. Upwork and Fiverr are closed to LIFE ZERO on *permission* grounds
(automated operation banned, recurring owner labour), not reachability — see `KNOWLEDGE_BASE.md`
K-006 and the Desk's route register. **Reading a board is not selling on it.** Do not expect revenue
from this gate; expect the ability to tell whether the 70% bet is still the right one.

## Kill condition

If two cycles of restored measurement do not change any allocation decision, the demand-research
function should be cut rather than kept running — and that is itself a useful result.
