## Requirements form (what the buyer fills in after ordering)

1. What should this spreadsheet do for you, in one or two sentences?
2. Who will use it — just you, or a team?
3. What do you want to be able to see at a glance when you open it?
4. Please attach a sample of your data (even 10 rows, with anything sensitive removed), or
   describe the columns you keep today.
5. Which do you actually work in — Excel, Google Sheets, or both?
6. Are there calculations, rates or fees it must apply? Please give the exact rules.
7. Is there a date range, a category list or a set of statuses it should use?
8. What are you doing today that you want to stop doing by hand?
9. Anything it must *not* do, or any deadline I should know about?

## FAQ

**Will it work in Google Sheets as well as Excel?**
Yes. I build to what both support — no macros and no Excel-only dynamic-array functions — and I
open the finished file in both before I send it.

**What if it isn't what I wanted?**
That is what the revision round is for. If the brief turns out to need something I've listed as
out of scope, you get refunded rather than handed a compromise.

**Can you work with my existing messy spreadsheet?**
Yes — that is the Standard and Premium packages. Send it as it is; you do not need to tidy it
first.

**Do I need to buy software?**
No. Excel 2016 or newer, or a free Google account.

**Is my data safe?**
Send a sample with names and numbers replaced if you would rather. I only need the shape of the
data to build against it.

**Can you file my taxes / connect to my bank?**
No. These are calculation and tracking tools, not filing services, and they don't connect to
live accounts.
